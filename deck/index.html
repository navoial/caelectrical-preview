Cloudflare + Astro

  • Pages are built before anyone visits, so they open fast on mobile data when a homeowner taps your ad or search result.
  • Photos are resized and their location data removed. Only the city is shown.
  • Cloudflare serves the pages from servers close to each visitor and helps block attacks and bots.
  • A small Cloudflare function receives the forms and filters spam.

A normal week, step by step.

What happens when a customer reaches out, when the crew finishes a job, and when you change an offer.

A homeowner needs help

  1. Finds you on Google. The page opens right away, even on mobile data.
  2. Taps Call, or sends the 3-step form: service, ZIP, phone.
  3. Cloudflare checks the form for spam.
  4. A form request lands in your monday.com Leads board with the page and the ad link, when there is one. The office gets a monday notification.

You see which pages and ads bring requests and call-button taps.

The crew finishes a job

  1. The office marks it Done in monday.com and adds before and after photos, the service, the city and a short note.
  2. Ticks Show on website.
  3. Cloudflare builds the project page. Address and customer name stay in monday.
  4. Published automatically, usually within minutes, on its own page, the home page and the city page.

Every real job adds local proof for that city.

You change an offer

  1. Open Sanity and sign in with Google.
  2. Change the amount, the text or the end date. Fields only, so the design can't break.
  3. Press Publish.
  4. The site updates within minutes. Offers stop showing after their end date.

No developer needed for everyday changes.

If something goes wrong: every published version is saved, so we can bring the previous one back in one click. Sanity keeps your recent text edits. Bigger changes are checked on a private preview link first.
