Questions before you call

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. An electrician comes out, finds the problem and gives you a written price. You decide after that. If you say no, you owe nothing.

How fast can you come for an emergency?

We answer the phone 24/7 and send the closest electrician. In most of Santa Clara County that is the same day, often within an hour or two.

How much does a panel upgrade cost?

It depends on whether you need a new panel on your current service or a full 200A service upgrade with PG&E. We give you a written price after a free look. Check the current offers above.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. We file the permit with your city and meet the inspector at the end. You get the signed-off permit for your records.

Can you install my Tesla or other EV charger?

Yes. We check your panel load first. Many homes can add a charger without a service upgrade, and we tell you honestly if yours can.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. California CSLB license #1035160, C-10 electrical, active, bonded, with workers' compensation insurance. You can check it on the CSLB website.
